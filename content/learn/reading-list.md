## How to use this list

These pages give you a map. The books below give you the depth. You do not need all of them. Read the first two, then select by the area where your reviews in this app are weakest.

## Core

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals of Software Architecture | Mark Richards, Neal Ford | The base of this app: characteristics, styles, trade-offs, and the role. Start here |
| Designing Data-Intensive Applications | Martin Kleppmann | How databases, replication, partitioning, and streams really work. The standard text for the data pages |

## Distributed systems and services

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Software Architecture: The Hard Parts | Neal Ford, Mark Richards, Pramod Sadalage, Zhamak Dehghani | How to split services and data, with sagas and trade-off analysis in detail |
| Building Microservices | Sam Newman | Practical design of services: boundaries, communication, deployment |
| Release It! | Michael Nygard | How systems fail in production, and the patterns that prevent it. The source of "circuit breaker" and "bulkhead" as software terms |

## Design and domain

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Domain-Driven Design | Eric Evans | Bounded contexts and a shared language with the business. Long, and the second half matters most to architects |
| A Philosophy of Software Design | John Ousterhout | A short book on complexity and how to make modules that hide it |

## Operations

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Site Reliability Engineering | Betsy Beyer and others, Google | Service levels, error budgets, and alerts. Free to read online |
| Accelerate | Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim | The evidence that links delivery practice to business results |

## People and organization

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| Team Topologies | Matthew Skelton, Manuel Pais | How team structure and architecture shape each other |
| The Software Architect Elevator | Gregor Hohpe | How an architect connects the engineers with the management |

## For interviews

| Book | Authors | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|
| System Design Interview | Alex Xu | Worked answers to the classic questions, in the style that interviews expect |

## Free on the web

- [martinfowler.com](https://martinfowler.com/architecture/), a large collection of articles on architecture
- [The Google SRE book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/), full text
- [microservices.io](https://microservices.io/patterns/), a catalog of patterns for services
- [The C4 model](https://c4model.com/), for diagrams
- [Architectural Katas](http://www.architecturalkatas.com), the original kata site of Ted Neward
